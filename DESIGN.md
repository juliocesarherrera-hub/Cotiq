# Cotiq Interface Design

## Direction

An operational workspace for Mexican contractors and independent service providers. The interface uses a clear familiar navigation, a deep evergreen brand header, and a softly tinted work area.

## Palette and surfaces

- Paper white work surfaces and a pale green-tinted workspace ground.
- A deep evergreen header gives the app a recognizable brand anchor.
- Deep evergreen ink carries primary text; muted green-gray supports secondary information.
- Emerald identifies the primary action, selection, and positive business states.
- Amber and rose are reserved for warnings and errors.

## Typography and hierarchy

- Use a clear sans-serif hierarchy with compact, bold page titles and comfortably readable supporting text.
- Keep monetary values and quote identifiers visually distinct from supporting labels.
- Avoid decorative all-caps tracking for routine copy.

## Layout and components

- Preserve the familiar top navigation and the existing quote, client, catalog, company, and license areas.
- Keep page content in a consistent centered workspace with responsive gutters.
- Use white work surfaces over the tinted ground, with restrained borders and 12–16px radii.
- Favor readable lists, forms, filters, and summary rows over decorative cards.
- Make empty states useful: explain the next task and provide a direct action without inventing customer data.
- Keep existing button, input, focus, loading, disabled, error, and empty-state behavior intact.

## Responsive behavior

- Navigation may scroll horizontally on narrow viewports while keeping every destination reachable.
- Summary values stack on small screens; primary actions remain visible and touch-friendly.
- Dense navigation and summary information reflow without horizontal page overflow.
